Privacy & Cookie Policy

LAST UPDATED: 11 OCTOBER 2026 · OPERATOR: krithikreddy138-arch · APPLIES TO: spark-krithikreddy138-8507.vercel.app

The short version: SPARK has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party scripts. We do not receive, collect, sell or share any personal data. The only thing SPARK stores is your saved ideas, and that stays in your own browser.

1. What we collect

We collect nothing from you. There is no sign-up, no login, no newsletter form, no contact form and no payment step, so there is no point at which you give us personal data.

SPARK makes no network requests after the page loads. There is no analytics beacon, no error reporter, no font CDN and no advertising tag. We could not build a profile of you even if we wanted to, because no data leaves your browser.

2. Data minimisation — what is stored, and where

Under data-minimisation principles we store only what the product actually needs. That is two keys in your browser's localStorage, both of which you control:

KeyContentsWhyHow long
spark-saved A list of idea numbers you bookmarked (for example [26,38]) So your saved collection survives a page reload Until you delete it
spark-consent One word: granted or denied So we can remember your privacy choice and stop asking Until you delete it

No names, email addresses, IP addresses, device identifiers, location, or browser fingerprint is collected or transmitted by SPARK. Idea numbers are not linked to any identity — there is nothing on our side to link them to.

3. Cookie policy

SPARK sets no cookies. Not first-party, not third-party, not "strictly necessary", not session or persistent cookies. You can confirm this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, look at the Cookies and Storage panels, and you will see nothing attributed to this site.

Because there are no cookies, there is nothing to opt out of and no cookie wall to bypass. When the consent banner offers CONTINUE WITHOUT SAVING, that choice genuinely disables local storage rather than being a decorative button.

5. Children's data and age consent

SPARK is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Because we collect no personal data at all, there is nothing of theirs for us to hold.

Age requirement: you must be 13 or older to use SPARK. This is stated in the consent banner before you interact with the site. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used SPARK, contact us and we will help — though in practice, deleting the browser's local storage removes everything associated with their use.

For teenagers aged 13 to 17, the same applies: we hold no data, and any data they store stays on their own device.

6. Data deletion requests

You can erase everything SPARK has ever stored about you in one step, right now, without contacting anyone.

  • In the product: scroll to the footer and press DELETE ALL LOCAL DATA. This immediately clears both keys above, empties your saved collection, and re-shows the consent banner.
  • In your browser: open site settings and clear data for this site, or run localStorage.clear() in the developer console.

Because SPARK holds no server-side copy of anything, deleting on your device is a complete deletion — there is no backup, archive or shadow record to request afterwards. There is no retention period to wait out.

If you would like written confirmation or have any other request, open an issue on our contact page and we will respond.

7. Emails and unsubscribe

SPARK sends no emails. There is no newsletter, no marketing list, no account notification and no receipt, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from and no unsubscribe link is needed. If we ever add email, every message will carry a working one-click unsubscribe link and we will update this policy before doing so.

8. Legal bases and your rights

Since no personal data is processed, most statutory data-protection rights have nothing to operate on. Still, wherever you live, you retain the right to ask us what we hold about you — the honest answer is nothing — and the right to have it deleted, which the button in section 6 already achieves.

  • GDPR / UK GDPR: no personal data is processed, so there is no lawful basis to document and no supervisory authority to complain to us over. You may still contact your local authority.
  • CCPA / CPRA: we do not sell, share or "share" for cross-context behavioural advertising any personal information, and we have no notice at collection to give because there is nothing to collect.
  • POPIA, PIPEDA, PDPA and similar: the same position — no personal data is processed.

9. Changes to this policy

If SPARK's behaviour ever changes — for example if an account system or analytics were added — this policy will be updated before, not after, that change ships, and the date at the top of the page will change with it. Any significant change will also be surfaced in the interface itself. The current version is always the one published with the site.
