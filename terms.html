11. Accessibility statement

We want SPARK to be usable by as many people as possible, and we test against the WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines. Here is an honest account of where things stand.

What works

  • Keyboard navigation: every control — the generator, filters, team mode, save, copy, roadmap, and the consent banner — is reachable and operable with the keyboard alone, in a sensible tab order, with a visible focus indicator on each.
  • Skip link: the first tab stop on the page skips straight past the header to the main content.
  • Colour contrast: text and interface elements were measured against the WCAG contrast formula and meet the AA thresholds — 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and non-text UI components.
  • Not colour-only: selected filters, saved state and the active difficulty are marked with borders, weight and text as well as colour.
  • Structure and screen readers: one h1 per page with a sensible heading order, landmarks, labelled controls, aria-pressed on toggle buttons, and a live region announcing each newly generated idea.
  • Motion: animations are small and decorative. If your system is set to reduce motion, we honour it.
  • Zoom and reflow: the layout reflows down to 320px wide and holds up at 200% zoom without losing content.
  • No images: SPARK contains no images, so there is no alt text to get wrong — all visual elements are text and CSS.

Known limitations

  • Some decorative glyphs are pure symbols (hearts, arrows, asterisks). Where they convey an action, the button also carries a text label; where they are decorative they are hidden from screen readers.
  • Screen-reader announcements depend on your browser's own live-region support, which varies.

Feedback

Found a barrier? Please tell us at our contact page, describing what you were trying to do and what got in the way. We will fix it and update this statement.
